# Iago Creative Space — Brand Guidelines v1.0

> A working document for internal team, freelancers, and client-facing deliverables.

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Mission
We turn brands into stories worth watching — through video production, advertising, and documentation that earns attention without losing soul.

### Brand Personality (5 attributes)
| Attribute | We are… | We are NOT… |
|---|---|---|
| **Creative** | Imaginative, fresh perspectives | Random, gimmicky |
| **Trustworthy** | Reliable, deadline-respecting | Stiff, corporate |
| **Playful** | Warm, human, witty | Childish, unprofessional |
| **Craft-driven** | Detail-obsessed, technically sharp | Pretentious, gatekeeping |
| **Collaborative** | Client-as-partner, transparent | Servile, "yes-people" |

### One-line positioning
*"A creative studio that makes brand stories feel alive — for teams who want their work watched, not skipped."*

## 2. Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
The "iago" wordmark with "CREATIVE SPACE" arc-text — full-color version on light backgrounds.

### Variations (to be exported as a set)
| Version | Use Case |
|---|---|
| **Full color on cream/white** | Default — websites, decks, business cards |
| **Full color on dark teal** | Hero sections, dark mode, video end-cards |
| **Mono teal (#0E4D5C)** | Print where pink is unavailable, single-color stamps |
| **Mono pink (#D67AB1)** | Accent moments, stickers, merch |
| **Mono cream (#F4ECDD)** | Reversed on teal/black photography |
| **Icon-only (the "i" with dot)** | Favicon, social avatar, watermark on videos |

### Clear Space
Maintain padding equal to the **height of the "i" dot** on all sides. Never crowd the logo with text, buttons, or other graphics inside this zone.

### Minimum Size
- **Digital:** 80 px wide
- **Print:** 25 mm wide
- **Favicon/avatar (icon only):** 32 × 32 px minimum

### Logo Don'ts
- Don't stretch, skew, or rotate
- Don't recolor outside the approved palette
- Don't add drop shadows, glows, or strokes
- Don't place on busy photography without a solid overlay
- Don't reconstruct from scratch — always use the official file
- Don't put on backgrounds with insufficient contrast (test at 50% zoom)

## 3. Color System

### Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Deep Teal** | `#0E4D5C` | 14, 77, 92 | 85, 50, 40, 35 | Headlines, nav, footer, primary buttons |
| Accent | **Playful Pink** | `#D67AB1` | 214, 122, 177 | 12, 60, 0, 0 | CTAs, hover, highlights, brand moments |
| Soft Neutral | **Cream** | `#F4ECDD` | 244, 236, 221 | 3, 5, 13, 0 | Section backgrounds, cards |

### Secondary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | **Off-White** | `#FAFAF7` | Default page background |
| Ink | **Near-Black** | `#111418` | Body text, captions |
| Muted | **Warm Gray** | `#6B6B6B` | Metadata, secondary text |
| Border | **Soft Gray** | `#E5E1D8` | Dividers, card outlines |

### Functional Colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Success | `#2E7D5B` | Form success, positive metrics |
| Warning | `#D89B3C` | Form warnings |
| Error | `#C5453A` | Form errors |

### Color Ratios (rule of thumb)
- **60%** Cream/Off-white (background)
- **25%** Deep Teal (text + structure)
- **10%** Near-black (body copy)
- **5%** Playful Pink (accent only — never wallpaper it)

### Pink Discipline
Pink is the **brand spark**, not the wallpaper. Use it for: CTAs, key statistics, hover states, the underline of an active nav item, one prop in a hero photo. If a page has more than ~5% pink coverage, it starts looking like a candy brand instead of a studio.

## 4. Typography

### Type Pairing

| Role | Font | Source | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** | DM Serif Display | Google Fonts (free) | Playfair Display, Georgia |
| **Body** | Inter | Google Fonts | system-ui, sans-serif |
| **Mono (specs/code)** | JetBrains Mono | Google Fonts | monospace |

> **Rev 1.1 — typography revision:** swapped from Clash Display (curvy display sans) to DM Serif Display (high-contrast Didone serif) to match the editorial gravitas of refined studio brands. The serif gives the headline a magazine-cover feel while the playful logo carries the brand's warmth.

### Type Scale (Desktop / Mobile)

| Token | Use | Size | Weight | Line-height | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `display-xl` | Hero headline | 96px / 56px | 600 | 1.0 | -0.03em |
| `display-lg` | Section headline | 72px / 40px | 600 | 1.05 | -0.02em |
| `display-md` | Subsection | 48px / 32px | 600 | 1.1 | -0.02em |
| `heading-lg` | H2 | 32px / 24px | 600 | 1.2 | -0.01em |
| `heading-md` | H3 | 24px / 20px | 600 | 1.3 | 0 |
| `body-lg` | Lead paragraph | 20px / 18px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 |
| `body` | Default body | 16px / 16px | 400 | 1.6 | 0 |
| `body-sm` | Caption, metadata | 14px / 14px | 400 | 1.5 | 0.01em |
| `eyebrow` | Section labels | 12px / 12px | 600 | 1.4 | 0.12em (uppercase) |

### Pairing Rules
- **Never** use DM Serif Display for body text (its thin strokes break up under 18px)
- **Never** use Inter for hero headlines (it's too neutral for an emotive studio brand)
- Use DM Serif Display Italic for the **ampersand** and the occasional emphasis word — its italic forms are the editorial flourish of the brand

## 5. Voice & Tone

### Voice Attributes
| We sound… | Example |
|---|---|
| **Confident, not cocky** | "We shipped 47 brand films last year." (not "We're the best in town") |
| **Warm, not corporate** | "Let's make something good." (not "Let us provide a tailored solution") |
| **Specific, not vague** | "3-day shoot, 6 deliverables, ROAS 4.2x" (not "comprehensive video services") |
| **Curious, not preachy** | "What's the story behind this product?" (not "You need video to succeed") |

### Tone Matrix (voice flexes by context)

| Context | Tone Dial | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | Playful + bold | "Make it watchable. Make it yours." |
| Case study intro | Confident + specific | "Rodalink wanted commuters to fall in love with bikes again. Here's how we did it." |
| Client email | Warm + professional | "Quick heads up — final cut is ready for your review. Link below." |
| Pricing page | Direct + honest | "Day rates start at IDR 25M. What you actually pay depends on scope — talk to us." |
| Social caption (IG) | Witty + visual-led | "BTS energy: 6am, 14°C, and Adi insists this shot is worth it. He was right." |
| Job listing | Genuine + inviting | "We're hiring a colorist who cares about skin tones the way we do." |
| 404 page | Self-aware | "This page got left on the cutting room floor. Try the menu." |

### Words We Use / Avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Craft, story, frame, cut | Synergy, leverage, holistic |
| Production, shoot, edit | Content (when we mean *video*) |
| Brief, scope, deliverable | Solution, offering |
| Watch, see, feel | Consume, engage |
| Studio, team | Agency (we're a *studio*) |

### Capitalization
- Headlines: **Sentence case** (not Title Case)
- Buttons: **Sentence case**
- Eyebrows/labels: **UPPERCASE** with letter-spacing

## 6. Visual Style

### Photography
**The look:** documentary realness, soft natural light, warm shadows. Behind-the-scenes energy over polished studio shots.

- Real people, real sets — not stock
- Slightly desaturated highlights, rich midtones
- Skin tones must read true (no orange/teal Instagram grade)
- Wide and medium shots > tight closeups (show the *space*)

### Video / Reel
- 16:9 for case study hero
- 9:16 vertical cuts for social
- Always include a poster frame (no black thumbnails)
- Reel music: cinematic but not generic — Indonesian artists where possible
- End-card: cream background, teal logo, pink "Iago.studio" URL

### Graphic Elements
- **Shapes:** rounded rectangles (16–24px radius), curved underlines, asymmetric blobs (echo the logo's curves)
- **Lines:** 2px teal, occasionally pink for accent
- **No:** harsh gradients, drop shadows, glassmorphism, sparkle effects

### Iconography
Use **Lucide** or **Phosphor** (regular weight) — 1.5px stroke, rounded line caps. Color: teal for default, pink on hover.

### Motion
- Ease curves: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (smooth, slightly bouncy)
- Hover transitions: 200ms
- Page transitions: 400ms
- Video thumbnail hover: 1.03 scale + subtle play icon fade-in
- **Never** auto-rotate carousels — let users control

## 7. Application Examples

### Button Styles

| Variant | Background | Text | Border | Hover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | `#D67AB1` (Pink) | White | none | Darken 10% |
| **Secondary** | `#0E4D5C` (Teal) | Cream | none | Lighten 8% |
| **Ghost** | transparent | Teal | 1.5px Teal | Teal bg, Cream text |
| **Link** | transparent | Teal | underline on hover | Pink underline |

All buttons: 12px vertical / 24px horizontal padding, 8px radius, 16px font, weight 600.

### Card Style
- Background: Cream `#F4ECDD`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 24px
- Border: none (use background contrast instead)
- Hover: lift 4px + shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(14, 77, 92, 0.08)`

### Form Inputs
- Background: White
- Border: 1.5px `#E5E1D8`
- Focus border: 1.5px `#0E4D5C` + 3px teal ring at 15% opacity
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 12px 16px

## 8. Quick Audit Checklist (for any new asset)

- [ ] Colors match the palette (no rogue pinks or teals)
- [ ] Clash Display only on headlines, Inter on body
- [ ] Logo has proper clear space + is an approved variation
- [ ] Pink coverage is ≤ 5% of the composition
- [ ] Body text ≥ 16px, contrast passes WCAG AA
- [ ] Photography looks documentary, not stock
- [ ] Tone matches the context (see matrix §5)
- [ ] No prohibited effects (drop shadows on logo, gradients, etc.)
- [ ] Client logos in case studies sit on their own neutral background
